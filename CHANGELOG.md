# What's new in Clawds of the Universe

The changes you'd notice in the Clawds of the Universe universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## Clawd Clash 4.2.0 (2026-10-29)

- **A new universe.** Clawds of the Universe joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds, Doctor Clawd, The Clawd Files, Stevie Clawdberg, Clawd of the Rings, Clawd Slayer, Stranger Clawds and Superclawdtural: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options. Or install it as its own cask, `brew install --cask clawds-of-the-universe`, which starts Clawd Clash there.
- **85 characters from the 1983 cartoon, She-Clawd and the 1987 film**, most with a signature move. He-Clawd's lightning on the Power Sword, Orkko's rain cloud, Man-At-Clawds' gauntlet, Man-E-Clawds' turning faces, Mekaneck's neck, Madame Razz falling off Broom, Kowl rolling his eyes and Gwildor's Cosmic Key. Facing them, Skeleclawd's cackle, Trap Clawd's chomping jaw, Two Bad arguing with himself, Faker's sparking ear, Hordakk's arm cannon, Mantenna's popping eyes and Horde Prime's hand out of the smoke. HEM turns up as a Master with a foam Power Sword.
- **21 places**, from Castle Greyskull, the Sorceress's chamber, the Royal Palace of Eternos and Man-At-Clawds' workshop to Snake Mountain, the Vine Jungle, the Mystic Mountains, Avion, Andreenos, Darksmoke, Trolla, the undersea kingdom, Beast Island and the House of Shokoti, then Etheria's Fright Zone, Bright Moon, Whispering Woods, Crystal Castle, Mystacor and Castle Chill, and a small town on Earth, each with something moving in it.
- **By the power of Greyskull.** When Prince Addam, Adorra, Cringer or Spirit compacts its context, lightning strikes and they become He-Clawd, She-Clawd, Battle Kat or Swift Wind, and change back at the next one. Nobody meets their other self on stage.
- **The rides**: the Wind Raider, Swift Wind, the Roton, the Horde skiff and Hordakk's rocket pick up whoever's done.
- **The moral of the story**: now and then a hero who finishes their work steps forward with a lesson.
- **Skeleclawd vanishes in a purple puff** after a fight ("I'll get you for this, He-Clawd!"), Orkko's spells misfire, Cringer and Gwildor hide behind the nearest hero, Imp turns into a stool and Stinkor clears the room. Ten practice drills of its own.
- **Twelve transitions**, each ending with the new place's name carved in stone: By the Power of Greyskull (the rarest), the Jawbridge, Zoar's Flight, Orkko's Trick, the Havoc Staff, the Crystal Orb, the Wind Raider, For the Honour of Greyskull, the Horde Bat, Shadow Weave, the Portal and Light Hope's Rainbow.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (He-Clawd and Skeleclawd, She-Clawd and Hordakk, Adorra and Katra, Teelah and Evil-Lynn…), and Skeleclawd and Hordakk never lead two sessions back to back.
- **Clawd Royale**: the Masters and the Horde join everyone else's cast in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, linked at the top of this page.
